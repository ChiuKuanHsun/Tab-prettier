Tab Prettier 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日

Tab Prettier 是一個 Chrome 擴充功能，把新分頁換成動漫合成畫和常用工具。開發者沒有伺服器，不蒐集、不保存、不販售任何使用者資料，也沒有任何分析或追蹤程式。

存在你電腦上的資料

以下資料只存在你的瀏覽器裡，開發者收不到：

  • 設定、常用連結、待辦事項、筆記、專注時要提醒的網站清單。若你開啟了 Chrome 同步，這些會透過 Google 帳號在你的裝置間同步，由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理。
  • 處理好的圖片（角色去背、景深圖）、收藏與隱藏的圖片清單、你上傳的圖片。圖片都在你的電腦上處理，不會上傳。
  • AniList 登入憑證（只在你登入 AniList 時）。

在你開啟對應功能並同意權限後，擴充功能會讀取瀏覽紀錄、開著的分頁、最近關閉的分頁和常去網站，用來顯示搜尋建議和連結面板。這些資料只在你的電腦上讀取，不會送出。

會送到第三方服務的資料

部分功能需要連線到下列服務。資料直接從你的瀏覽器送到該服務，不經過開發者：

服務送出的資料用途與時機
Wallhaven、Danbooru、Nekosia、waifu.im搜尋條件（場景、標籤、角色名稱，可能包含你 AniList 清單中的角色）取得背景和角色圖片
Open-Meteo大致位置（經緯度到小數點後兩位，或你輸入的城市名稱）開啟天氣時查詢天氣
ipwho.is／ipinfo.io只有連線本身（你的 IP 位址）天氣設為自動定位時推算城市
Google 搜尋建議你在搜尋框輸入的文字只在你開啟搜尋建議時
AniList登入憑證只在你登入後，讀取你自己的動畫清單

連線到任何網站時，你的 IP 位址和瀏覽器資訊都會被該網站看到。這些服務依各自的隱私權政策處理資料。

不會做的事

  • 不販售或轉交資料給第三方。
  • 不把資料用在與擴充功能本身無關的用途。
  • 不用於信用評估或放款。

刪除資料

在設定頁可以登出 AniList，或關閉天氣、搜尋建議等功能。移除擴充功能時，存在電腦上的資料會一併刪除；已透過 Chrome 同步的資料，可以在 Google 帳號的 Chrome 同步設定中清除。

政策變更與聯絡方式

政策若有變更，會更新這一頁和上方的生效日期。有任何問題，請寄信到 kuanhsunextension@gmail.com。

Tab Prettier Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Tab Prettier is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a composed anime scene and a set of everyday tools. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, store or sell any user data. The extension contains no analytics or tracking.

Data kept on your computer

The following stays in your browser and never reaches the developer:

  • Settings, pinned links, to-dos, notes and the sites you list for focus reminders. If you use Chrome sync, these sync between your devices through your Google account and are handled under Google's privacy policy.
  • Prepared pictures (character cut-outs, depth maps), your favourite and hidden pictures, and pictures you upload. All image processing happens on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
  • Your AniList sign-in token, only if you sign in to AniList.

When you turn on the matching feature and grant the permission, the extension reads your browsing history, open tabs, recently closed tabs and top sites to show search suggestions and the links panel. This is read on your computer only and never sent anywhere.

Data sent to third-party services

Some features connect to the services below. Data goes straight from your browser to the service, never through the developer:

ServiceData sentPurpose and when
Wallhaven, Danbooru, Nekosia, waifu.imSearch terms (scenes, tags, character names, which may include characters from your AniList list)Fetching background and character pictures
Open-MeteoApproximate location (latitude and longitude to two decimals, or the city you enter)Weather, when turned on
ipwho.is / ipinfo.ioOnly the connection itself (your IP address)Finding your city when weather is set to locate automatically
Google search suggestionsText you type in the search boxOnly when you turn on search suggestions
AniListSign-in tokenReading your own anime list, only after you sign in

Any website you connect to can see your IP address and browser details. These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

What we never do

  • Sell or transfer data to third parties.
  • Use data for anything unrelated to the extension's features.
  • Use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Deleting your data

You can sign out of AniList or turn off weather and search suggestions on the settings page. Removing the extension deletes the data stored on your computer; data synced through Chrome sync can be cleared in your Google account's Chrome sync settings.
